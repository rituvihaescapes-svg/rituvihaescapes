Trip Guidelines & Booking Policy

Everything you need to know before joining your Rituviha Escapes squad!

Respect & Good Vibes

Our stranger trips are meant for making friends and building a supportive squad. Respect fellow co-travelers, drivers, and local staff at all times.

Care For Personal Belongings

Keep your cash, mobiles, cameras, and personal items safe. Rituviha Escapes is not responsible for lost or damaged personal items during transit or stays.

Punctuality Matters

Please gather on time for train departures, 4x4 Jeep safaris, and group meal gatherings so the entire squad can enjoy every activity smoothly.

Eco-Friendly & Responsible Travel

Help keep pristine waterfalls and hill stations clean. Do not litter plastic or disturb wildlife in Chinnar or Eravikulam sanctuaries.

ADVANCE PAYMENT NON-REFUNDABLE

The advance amount of ₹2,000 per person is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.

Reason: Your advance is immediately utilized to reserve non-refundable IRCTC Sleeper train tickets, block private 4x4 Jeeps, and pay stay/campsite deposits.
